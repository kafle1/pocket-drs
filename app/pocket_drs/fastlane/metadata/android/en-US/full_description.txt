Pocket DRS gives you an LBW review from one phone clip. No licence to buy, no demo call to book, no special camera. Sign in with Google and check your first ball today.

Film the delivery from behind the bowler's stumps, mark both sets of stumps, and send it. For every ball you get:

• where it pitched
• where it hit the pad
• whether it was going on to hit the stumps, drawn over your own video and in 3D
• the call: out, not out, or umpire's call, with the reason

Straight about close calls
When it's too close to call, you get umpire's call. When a clip can't be judged, you get no call and the reason why, so you know what to change next time.

Works on any pitch
You mark the stumps yourself, so it works in the nets, at the club or in the backyard. Short or long pitch? Set the real pitch length in Settings.

What you need
• Anything that holds the phone still behind the bowler's stumps
• Both sets of stumps in the frame
• A red or pink ball
• The main camera lens, not the ultra-wide
• An internet connection, as the clip is checked on our server

Free every day
You get 5 free reviews every day. A ball that fails or gets no call doesn't count.

Privacy
Sign in with Google. No ads. The server deletes your clip as soon as it has checked it and the result after a day. You can delete your account at any time in Settings.

Pocket DRS is a coaching tool, not an umpire. It can't tell bat from pad, so a ball that hit the bat first isn't out whatever the call says.
