Pocket DRS

Was it out? Check the LBW from your phone.

Film one ball from behind the bowler's stumps. Pocket DRS tracks the ball and gives the call, with its path drawn on your own clip.

5 free reviews when you sign in. On iPhone, carry on right here in Safari.

Three steps, about half a minute

  1. Film the ball. Phone still, behind the bowler's stumps, camera at 60 frames a second.
  2. Mark the stumps. Tap the four corners of each set of stumps once.
  3. Get the call. Out, not out or umpire's call, with the speed and a 3D replay.

The same three checks as TV DRS

Where it pitched, where it hit the pad, and whether it was going on to hit the stumps. Too close to tell is umpire's call, like on TV. A clip that can't support a call gets no call, never a guess.

Trim and mark the stumps

Slide to the delivery and set where it starts and ends. Then tap the 8 stump corners on a clear frame.
